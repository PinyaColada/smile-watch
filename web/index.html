METHODOLOGY

Same delta.
Same time of day.

We recover European Black–Scholes IV from synthetic option quotes, then interpolate in spot-delta space to follow 15Δ and 25Δ puts and calls as their strikes move.

Each IV, risk reversal and wing is compared with the same minute after the New York open in the previous N completed sessions, excluding today; at least eight valid observations are required, and the band is ±1 sample standard deviation.

All market data are synthetic, including the deliberate 14:00 downside-skew injection in the final session; 30-second sampling repeats minute observations, half-day afternoons remain missing, and faint future surfaces are visible only as an ex-post replay aid.

This measures unusualness, not predictive value; the optional IBKR adapter defaults to paper mode and has NOT been tested against a live account.
